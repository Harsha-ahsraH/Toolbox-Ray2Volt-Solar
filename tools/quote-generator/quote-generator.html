About Ray2Volt Solar

Ray2Volt Solar Private Limited is a premier solar energy solutions provider committed to delivering high-quality, reliable, and efficient solar power systems. We specialize in residential, commercial, and industrial rooftop installations, offering transparent pricing, premium tier-1 components, and complete end-to-end project support.

Our mission is to accelerate the transition to sustainable energy by empowering homeowners and businesses with intelligent, high-performance solar solutions. From precision site surveys and seamless DISCOM net-metering approvals to expert commissioning and robust after-sales service, we handle everything so you can simply enjoy the savings.
