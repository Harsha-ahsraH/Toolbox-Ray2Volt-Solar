Technical Overview

Operation, Safety & Monitoring

How the System Works

01
Daytime Solar Production

Panels generate maximum energy when sunlight is available. The inverter continuously tracks the best operating point for higher generation.

02
Load Priority

Solar energy is consumed by connected loads first, reducing the power imported from the grid.

03
Surplus Export

When solar generation is higher than site demand, surplus units are exported through the bi-directional meter.

04
Night and Low-Sun Operation

At night or during low generation, the premises draw power from the grid as usual.

Key Technical Features

Module Performance

High-efficiency PV modules are selected for reliable output across seasonal conditions.

Inverter Intelligence

MPPT tracking, grid synchronization, event logging and fault protection are built into the inverter platform.

Electrical Safety

MCBs, isolators, surge protection and earthing reduce operational risk and simplify maintenance.

Remote Monitoring

Generation trends and inverter status can be monitored to spot abnormal performance early.

Battery-backed operation

For hybrid/off-grid systems, the battery stores surplus energy and supports selected backup loads when the grid is unavailable.

Grid-interactive operation

For on-grid systems, the grid remains available as backup supply while solar generation reduces daytime imports and can export surplus units.
