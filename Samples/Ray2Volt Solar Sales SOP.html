On-Grid Solar System
Connected directly to the electricity grid. Excess power earns you credit through Net Metering — no batteries needed, lowest cost.
How On-Grid Works
  • Solar panels generate electricity during the day, powering your home directly.
  • Excess power is exported to the grid — your meter runs backward, earning you credit.
  • At night, you consume power from the grid as usual — but your bill is offset by daytime credits.
  • No batteries required, so the system costs significantly less.
  • Installation is done without disturbing your existing house wiring.
Best for: Homes and businesses with reliable grid power and minimal power cuts. Maximum savings, zero maintenance headache.

Hybrid Solar System
Grid-connected + battery backup. Uninterrupted power 24/7 — solar during the day, lithium batteries at night.
How Hybrid Works
☀️ During the Day
  • Solar panels generate power for your home — power cuts don't affect you at all.
  • Excess solar energy is used to charge the batteries for later use.
  • Any remaining surplus is exported to the grid for net metering credits.
🌙 During the Night
  • Powerful lithium-ion batteries kick in automatically — running ACs, fridges, lights, and fans all night.
  • 90% Depth of Discharge — uses almost all stored energy, unlike lead-acid (50%).
  • Seamless switch — no flicker, no disruption, your appliances stay on.
⚡ During Power Cuts
  • Morning power cuts? Solar panels deliver power directly — you won't even notice.
  • Evening/night cuts? Batteries take over instantly, providing hours of backup.
  • Your family's comfort is never compromised — 24/7 uninterrupted power.
Best for: Areas with frequent power cuts. Ideal for families who want the peace of mind that their home runs smoothly round-the-clock — no inverter UPS hassles, no fuel generators.
